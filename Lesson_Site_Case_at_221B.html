Give your evidence: … because …

Form
subject + must / might / could / can't + verb

✓ He must be a teacher.   ✓ She might know.

He must to be   She musts be

It mustn't be Tom. → It can't be Tom.

Say it

Homework: be Sherlock at home

  1. Choose a desk, a bag or a shelf at home (ask the owner first!) or use the picture on your homework sheet.
  2. Write 6 deductions about the owner:
    2 × must   2 × might / could   2 × can't
  3. Every sentence needs because + your evidence.
  4. Bring your sheet to the next lesson. Your partner will guess who the owner is.
